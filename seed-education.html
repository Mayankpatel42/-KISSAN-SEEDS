Understand different seed technologies before making a decision.

🌱

Improved Varieties

Improved varieties are developed through plant breeding to provide useful characteristics such as better yield, disease resistance, or suitability to particular environments.

💡 Always choose a variety suitable for your region and farming conditions.
🌾

Hybrid Seeds

Hybrid seeds are produced by crossing selected parent plants to combine desirable characteristics.

💡 Hybrid performance can depend on crop, environment and management practices.
🧬

GM Technology

Genetically modified crops involve introducing specific genetic changes using biotechnology. Their use and approval depend on crop, country and regulatory systems.

⚠️ KisanSeed provides neutral educational information rather than promoting a specific technology.
🔬

Gene Editing

Gene-editing technologies can make targeted changes to an organism's genetic material. Different countries have different regulatory approaches.

💡 Farmers should rely on approved varieties and official agricultural guidance.
